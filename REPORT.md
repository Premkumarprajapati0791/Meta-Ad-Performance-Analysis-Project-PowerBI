Meta Ad Performance Analysis

📊 Dashboard Insights Report


📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

1️⃣ Overall Performance Summary

The advertising campaigns generated 339.8K impressions, resulting in 40.1K clicks and 46.1K total engagements.

Key conversion metrics:

1.CTR: 11.79% → Very strong top-of-funnel performance

2.Engagement Rate: 13.58% → Audience content resonance is high

3.Conversion Rate: 5.21% → Moderate lower-funnel efficiency

4.Purchase Rate: 0.61% → Weak final conversion from impressions

Insight:
Campaigns are highly effective at generating awareness and engagement, but purchase efficiency remains low. There is a noticeable drop-off between interest and final conversion.

This indicates a strong upper funnel (awareness & interest) but weak lower funnel (purchase action).


2️⃣ Engagement Breakdown
🔹 By Gender

1.Male: 25K (54.3%)

2.Female: 15.5K (34%)

3.Other/Not Specified: 4.5K (10%)

Insight:
Male users show higher engagement overall. However, further targeting refinement could improve female engagement and conversion potential.


🔹 By Target Age

1.Highest engagement: 20–30 age group

2.Engagement drops significantly after 35+

Insight:
Young adults (18–30) represent the core audience segment. Campaigns should prioritize this demographic for improved ROI.

🎯 Final Business Insights & Recommendations

1.Upper funnel is strong (high CTR & Engagement Rate).

2.Lower funnel is weak (low purchase rate 0.61%).

3.Core audience: 18–30 age group.

4.Peak engagement time: Afternoon & Evening.

5.High-potential markets: India & US.


Recommended Actions:

1.Improve landing page optimization

2.Implement retargeting campaigns

3.Enhance conversion-focused creatives

4.Shift more budget toward high-performing geographies

5.Schedule ads during peak engagement hours

6.Optimize offers and checkout experience
